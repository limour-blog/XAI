# 参考文献

完整条目见原始 [ICML.bbl](../../../TeX/In-Context-Learning/arXiv-2212.07677v2/ICML.bbl) 和 [BibTeX 数据库](../../../TeX/In-Context-Learning/arXiv-2212.07677v2/example_paper.bib)。以下条目与本文的主要机制直接相关：

- Vaswani et al. (2017), [Attention Is All You Need](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762) — 自注意力架构。
- Schlag, Irie & Schmidhuber (2021), [Linear Transformers Are Secretly Fast Weight Programmers](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/schlag21a.html) — 线性注意力和 fast weights。
- Finn, Abbeel & Levine (2017), [Model-Agnostic Meta-Learning for Fast Adaptation of Deep Networks](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/finn17a.html) — 基于梯度的元学习。
- Olsson et al. (2022), [In-context Learning and Induction Heads](../../arXiv-2209.11895v1/INDEX.MD) — 前缀匹配与复制。
- Garg et al. (2022), [What Can Transformers Learn In-Context? A Case Study of Simple Function Classes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.01066) — 简单函数类上的上下文学习。
- Akyürek et al. (2023), [What Learning Algorithm Is In-Context Learning? Investigations with Linear Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.15661) — 线性模型及并行的梯度下降构造。
