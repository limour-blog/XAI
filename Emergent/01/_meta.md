## Abstract

**Background:** Scaling often improves language modeling loss predictably, yet some downstream scores appear flat before rising above chance. **Methods:** The survey uses training FLOPs and parameter counts to compare few-shot tasks and benefits from augmented prompting or fine-tuning. **Results:** Eight few-shot examples and four intervention examples illustrate the pattern. Cross-entropy already improves at small scales on six BIG-Bench tasks; a 62B PaLM model outperforms near-random larger models on 14 tasks. **Conclusion:** The observed scale depends on model, data, training and evaluation; these examples do not imply a universal threshold.
