## Abstract

Intermediate decoding length controls the power of log-precision transformers. Linear decoding can recognize all regular languages with projected pre-norm, while logarithmic decoding is in log-space and linear decoding is in linear space. Under the specified architecture, polynomially many decoding steps characterize P via real-time multitape Turing machine simulation.
