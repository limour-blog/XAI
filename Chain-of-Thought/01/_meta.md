## Abstract

Few-shot exemplars with natural-language reasoning before the answer improve arithmetic, commonsense and symbolic reasoning in sufficiently large models without finetuning. With eight exemplars, PaLM 540B improves from 17.9% to 56.9% on GSM8K. Control prompts that only add tokens or move reasoning after the answer do not reproduce the gains.
